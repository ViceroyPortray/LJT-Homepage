I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate in the HKUST NLP Group, supervised by Professor Junxian He, working on natural language processing and machine learning.

## Academic Background

- **Ph.D. in Computer Science**, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (2024-Present); Ph.D. candidate at the HKUST NLP Group, supervised by Professor Junxian He.
- **B.Eng.**, Shanghai Jiao Tong University (2020-2024), graduated in June 2024.
- Recipient of the **Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship**, awarded by Shanghai Jiao Tong University.
